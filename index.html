What is required before games can actually stream?

☁️ Streaming is not connected yet

A website cannot run demanding PC games merely by displaying a game card. A working cloud gaming experience needs a game host or compatible streaming provider.

  1. Choose a legitimate cloud gaming or remote-play service that supports your device and the game you want.
  2. Check its current price, availability in your region, playtime limits and supported games.
  3. Connect through the provider's supported integration or launch its official service.
  4. Test your network and controls before expecting smooth gameplay.

Free and unlimited: This cannot be guaranteed. Free tiers may have queues, session limits or restricted game libraries. CloudVex does not currently provide a gaming server.
